THE STRUCTURE

A week with a backbone.

Four gym anchors, three runs, one optional pump. Miss a day? Resume the sequence; don't cram.

THE RULES

Progress without guessing.

Hold main lifts steady. Add a small load only when all sets meet the target with at least two reps left. If recovery is low, trim a set and back off the weight. Pain or illness means no hard training. Accessory variation changes at most once a fortnight.

Lower is a new, conservative fourth core day. Your former Pull-day deadlift moves there; the rest stays familiar. The Short Pump remains optional.
